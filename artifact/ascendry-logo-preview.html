ASCENDRY / corrected monogram study

Rise to a peak.

Each direction is a true A: the legs visibly converge as they rise, while two geometric summit planes remain separate. The higher right summit holds the “ascend” cue; the lower, one-sided bar avoids an H-shaped bridge.

01 / Three converging-A variations

Recommended

01 — Split Summit

The most open peak gap and strongest rise. The detached low bar attaches only at the left plane.

Alternative

02 — Narrow Summit

A tighter interior creates a refined, slightly more compact silhouette for small applications.

Alternative

03 — Monument

Broader planes and a lower left apex make this the most architectural reading.
